# Risk Warnings

Warnings help the user notice costs or evidence gaps. They are not legal conclusions.

## Warning Levels

### Information Missing

Use when the balance cannot be reconstructed because a material fact is absent:

- actual amount advanced;
- full repayment history;
- signed IOU or repayment plan;
- compensation or assignment date;
- fee rate or calculation base.

Show the exact document or transaction needed next.

### Balance Difference

Use when the platform or collector's claimed balance does not equal the reconstructed amount. Display:

- claimed balance and claim date;
- reconstructed known amount;
- unexplained difference;
- whether missing payments or missing fees could move the result in either direction.

Do not call the difference an unlawful charge before identifying its source.

### Cost And Structure Attention

Flag, with the relevant clause and charging party:

- interest, guarantee, insurance or service fees calculated on different bases;
- penalty interest, liquidated damages and other fees charged concurrently;
- compounding of unpaid interest or penalty interest;
- remaining future fees becoming due after acceleration;
- a fixed percentage penalty based on original principal;
- payment allocation that reduces fees before principal;
- compensation, subrogation or assignment changing the creditor;
- charges described as actual costs without invoices or amount evidence;
- duplicated-looking charges collected by different parties.

### Disclosure Attention

For loan business subject to the rules effective 2026-08-01, compare the signed package against the `个人贷款综合融资成本明示表`. It should identify principal, each interest/fee item, charging method, standard and charging party, normal all-in annualized cost, and contingent costs such as overdue penalties. Charges outside the disclosed list require evidence and review.

For older loans, do not assume the 2026 rule applied retroactively. Still compare marketing, contract, repayment plan and actual charges for consistency.

## Rate Calculations

Calculate at least two measures when the data permits:

1. `nominal contract rate` from the loan contract;
2. `all-in annualized cost` using actual cash received and all required repayments/fees, preferably by IRR methodology.

Use actual cash received rather than face principal when a fee was deducted before disbursement.

Also combine concurrently calculated overdue rules only when they share a comparable base. Show their annualized equivalent as a cost signal, while preserving the original daily, monthly or annual contract unit in the evidence record.

Trigger a concise high-cost reminder when:

- an all-in annualized cost reaches a sourced case-specific attention line;
- the all-in annualized cash-flow result exceeds the nominal rate by at least five percentage points, indicating that fees or schedule assumptions need review;
- concurrent overdue rates on the same base annualize above the normal all-in cost or a sourced overdue attention line.

These are review triggers, not automatic legal conclusions.

## Legal-Limit Guardrail

Do not use a single 24%, 36% or four-times-LPR threshold as a universal rule for every online loan.

- The Supreme People's Court four-times one-year LPR judicial-protection rule concerns private lending and depends on the contract date and transaction type.
- Banks, consumer-finance companies, trusts and other licensed lenders may be governed by different rules and case-specific judicial review.
- A platform may involve a licensed lender plus guarantee, insurance and service parties; classify the parties before selecting any legal comparison.

Use wording such as:

- `综合融资成本显著偏高，建议核对收费依据`;
- `多项逾期费用并行，需检查计费基数和是否重复`;
- `可能超过适用标准，需结合贷款主体、合同日期和当地裁判口径复核`;
- `无法仅凭当前材料判断是否合法或是否受司法保护`.

Never output `illegal`, `usury`, `invalid` or `need not repay` as an automated conclusion.
